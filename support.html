How ordering works

  1. 1. Add products to your cart

    Only in-stock, available products can be ordered.

  2. 2. Submit your order

    You will receive an order number (e.g. CC-000001) and payment instructions.

  3. 3. Pay and upload proof

    Pay using an approved method, then upload your payment proof.

  4. 4. Verification

    Our team manually verifies every payment. An uploaded screenshot alone does not confirm payment.

  5. 5. Fulfilment

    Once confirmed, your order is processed and prepared for pickup or delivery.
